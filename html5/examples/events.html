Events and reading the player

The player tag tells your page what happens. vs:ready hands you the player, which is a Video.js player, so all standard Video.js events work. Play, pause, seek and change the quality, and watch the log. Events reach your page only with the player tag: inside an iframe they stay in the iframe.
