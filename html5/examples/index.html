ViewStream HTML5 Player SDK Workbench Configurator Examples עברית

Examples

Each page is one small, working example. The code it runs is printed on the page, ready to copy. The examples play ViewStream's sample content until you put your own tenant in kit.config.js.

How to connect your tenant and how the settings work: docs/TENANT-AND-CONFIG.md (עברית).

  • Player tag

    The smallest embed: one tag and a stream address.

  • iframe

    The recommended embed for your own channel. Needs your tenant.

  • Live channel

    A live channel with the programme guide, start-over and rewind. Plays your channel when your tenant is set.

  • Video with subtitles, chapters and thumbnails

    Tag attributes and data-options.

  • Player configuration

    Turning features on and off: the same keys you set in ViewStream Studio, tried from the page.

  • Ads (VAST)

    A pre-roll with a skip button, from a VAST tag.

  • Events and reading the player

    Listening to the player and reading its state from your page.

  • Preview tiles

    Muted previews that play on hover, for lists and home pages.
