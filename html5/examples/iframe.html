iframe

The recommended embed. ViewStream chooses the stream and the settings, so player updates and stream changes reach your site with no change on your side. The address names your tenant and what to play.

Put your tenant and channel in kit.config.js to see your channel here. The code below shows where they go.

This plays your real channel through ViewStream's hosted page, so it counts as a viewing in your statistics.

The address


    
  
  • /e/<tenant>/live/<channel> for a live channel
    • 
  
  • /e/<tenant>/vod/<video id> for a video
    • 
  
  • /e/<tenant>/clip/<clip id> for a clip
    • 



Options after ?: lang=he, en, ru or ar;
autoplay=1 (starts muted); config=<name> for a named player configuration;
start=<seconds> (not for live). ViewStream Studio writes the whole iframe for you: open a channel,
clip or video and use Embed & share.
