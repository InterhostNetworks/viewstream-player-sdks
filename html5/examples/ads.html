Ads (VAST)

Press play: a pre-roll runs before the video, with a skip button, a "Learn more" link and a countdown. The ad comes from a ViewStream demo VAST tag. If an ad fails or does not start in time, the video plays.

Nothing about ads goes on your page. Turn ads on and set your VAST or VMAP tags in your player configuration in
ViewStream Studio, under Players, with the same ads.* keys as above. Other demo tags to try in
default_tag: ads/vast/pre-noskip.xml (no skip button) and ads/vast/vmap-all.xml
(pre, mid and post-roll; add "mid" and "post" to positions).
